# Thruk is easy to use

Thruk includes:

- [Ready-to-use packages](/download.html)
- Full integration into OMD ([omdistro.org](http://omdistro.org))
- Flexible, modular design
- 2 themes included
- Easy to extend with your own plugins

Thruk developers work with large monitoring installations every day.
They focus on usability and performance.
Thruk is designed for installations with thousands of hosts and services.
