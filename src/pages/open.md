# Thruk is free and open source

Thruk is a community-driven project with free access to its source code.
It has no open-core edition or hidden features.

Thruk uses the same license terms as Perl 5: Artistic License 1.0 or GNU GPL version 1 or later.
See the [license terms](https://github.com/sni/Thruk/blob/master/LICENSE).

Share your experience and help other users on the [mailing list](https://groups.google.com/group/thruk).
Experienced users can also contribute small pull requests on [GitHub](http://github.com/sni/Thruk) or create plugins.
